🌿 Physics ID: QM-092

What is the time-dependent Schrödinger Equation?

Describe its components and physical significance.

🌿 Physics
iℏ(∂Ψ/∂t) = ĤΨ
  • i: imaginary unit
  • ℏ: reduced Planck constant
  • Ψ (Psi): wave function of the quantum system
  • t: time
  • Ĥ: Hamiltonian operator (total energy)
Significance It dictates how the quantum state of a physical system changes over time.
